Expert Care for Your Home's Shield

From high-pressure cleaning to specialized bird proofing, we provide premium exterior maintenance services designed for longevity and curb appeal.

Core Service

High-Pressure / Soft Wash Cleaning

We provide both high-pressure cleaning and soft washing, with the most appropriate method selected based on the roof material, age, condition and level of contamination. Our qualified technicians assess the roof and recommend the suitable washing method for the best result.

Restoration

Roof Painting

Transform and protect your home with our premium roof coating systems. We use high-build, UV-resistant paints specifically formulated for the harsh South Pacific climate.

Structural

Roof Repointing

Cracked ridge capping leads to leaks. Our flexible pointing compound ensures your roof stays watertight while moving naturally with temperature changes.

Maintenance

Gutter Guard Installation

Say goodbye to seasonal ladder climbing. Our premium mesh systems keep leaves out while allowing maximum water flow, protecting your foundation from overflow damage.

Exterior Care

House Washing

A full exterior soft wash removes dirt, cobwebs, and road grime, instantly boosting your property value and making your home feel like new again.

Protection

Bird Proofing

Keep solar panels and roof cavities free from nesting pests. Our humane and effective bird proofing solutions prevent structural damage and messy solar efficiency loss.

Hygiene

Gutter Cleaning

Thorough removal of leaves, dirt and debris from gutters to help maintain clear water flow and reduce the risk of overflow during heavy rain.
